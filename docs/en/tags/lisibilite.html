Kerberos 1.4

A text serif in the Kis-Janson line, rare among free fonts: four variable axes (weight 300-700, optical size 8-72 pt, grade, lowercase height), small caps, ligatures, Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. For long reading on screen or in PDFs. OFL 1.1, a young project (Oct 2026).

Vavin 1.1

A Garamond for screens: derived from EB Garamond, with an x-height raised by 16% (0.72 of the cap height). Nine styles in three families, including a condensed and a display one, 503 Latin languages. Readable at small sizes, still classic. OFL 1.1, version 1.1 out Oct 2026.

Agràve Pro

A handwriting face designed for longer texts: open counters, even strokes, small caps and figure sets. Variable (weight 100-700), it was just merged into the Google Fonts repository. For a handwritten touch that stays readable. OFL 1.1, version 1.100, a 2026 project.

LXGW WenKai 1.530

A free Chinese kai-style font derived from Fontworks' Klee One, popular for reading. 1.530 (Oct 17) adds 50 Unicode 18 small seal script characters, mahjong and dice symbols, completes JIS X 0213 kanji and redraws hundreds of glyphs. OFL 1.1.

Libron 0.31

A reading serif derived from Readerly (itself based on Newsreader), hand-edited in FontForge for a calmer texture. Ready-made builds for Kobo, KOReader, Kindle, desktop and web. 0.31 (Oct 9) adds fractions, superscripts and Unicode spaces. OFL, in active development.

Found via: GitHub — nicoverbruggen/libron v0.31

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

2024 version of the Braille Institute's hyperlegible typeface: new glyphs, revised kerning, 6 weights with italics (versus 2). OFL 1.1, sources on GitHub, on Google Fonts. Ideal for UI, subtitles and docs.
