Libron 0.31

A reading serif derived from Readerly (itself based on Newsreader), hand-edited in FontForge for a calmer texture. Ready-made builds for Kobo, KOReader, Kindle, desktop and web. 0.31 (Oct 9) adds fractions, superscripts and Unicode spaces. OFL, in active development.

2024 version of the Braille Institute's hyperlegible typeface: new glyphs, revised kerning, 6 weights with italics (versus 2). OFL 1.1, sources on GitHub, on Google Fonts. Ideal for UI, subtitles and docs.
